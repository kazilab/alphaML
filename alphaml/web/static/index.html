Privacy notice

How this alphaML server handles personal data, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

This server is run by its operator. Contact: ask the administrator of this server.

What is stored, and why

  • Your email address and a salted hash of your password, to run your account and to send the password-reset links you ask for. They are kept until you delete your account.
  • The data sets you upload and the results of your analyses, to run the analyses you ask for.
  • One login cookie, needed to keep you logged in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Your network address is used briefly, in memory only, to limit failed logins and password-reset requests. It is not stored.

Your data is not shared, sold or used for any other purpose. Password-reset emails are sent through an email provider, and a network provider (such as Cloudflare) may process connection data when it delivers this site.

Legal basis

Your consent, and providing the service you ask for (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and (b)).

Do not upload personal or sensitive data

Upload only anonymised data. Do not upload anything that identifies a person, such as names, dates of birth, or patient or sample identifiers that can be linked to a person, and no special categories of personal data such as health or genetic data about identifiable people (GDPR Article 9). You are responsible for the data you upload and for having the right to use it.

Your rights

You can download your results, delete data sets and jobs at any time, and delete your account with all its data from the account menu, which also withdraws your consent. You have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country. For anything else, use the contact above.

The regulation

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation): eur-lex.europa.eu.

Upload expression data and labels and choose feature selection. AutoML finds the best algorithm, tunes it and explains the model; or pick an algorithm yourself.

1Data set

CSV or TSV files with sample names in the first column. Data files have one column per feature (e.g. gene); the labels file has one column per target (e.g. a drug).

3Feature selection

Methods run in sequence, each on the features kept by the previous one.

4Model

AutoML cross-validates every selected algorithm, tunes the best ones with Optuna within the time budget, tries a soft-voting ensemble of them, and keeps the best candidate.
